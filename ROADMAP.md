# Untitled Armies roadmap

The immediate priority is the campaign loop: recruit, fight, recover, and improve the company. Side quests follow after that loop is tested in landscape on a phone.

## 1. Party and promotions

- Hire and promote selected quantities. Show available recruits, healthy troops, wounded troops, training progress, price, and wages.
- Award troop training from deployed survivors. Resolve casualties and training together once per battle.
- Give inns one treatment budget for the whole company. Show the recovery preview before resting.
- Verify that older saves retain their troop types, party size, wounds, equipment, and campaign progress.

## 2. Branching troop trees

- Pilot Valdoria first. Valdorian recruits can choose a shield, spear, or bow path. House retainers have a mounted path.
- Make each path change what troops do in battle. Verify that existing faction and tier combinations still work.
- Extend the same model to the other realms after the pilot has been played and balanced.

## 3. Deployment and reserves

- Offer an optional deployment phase and keep Quick Start.
- Choose which ready troops deploy, which wait in reserve, and where formations start on the player's side.
- Test the same setup and commands in Classic practice and campaign. Keep practice isolated from campaign saves.

## 4. Weapons, armor, and loot

- Expand the weapon designer with readable reach, handling, damage, and weight tradeoffs.
- Let equipment serve as gear, sale goods, or salvage for company kits.
- Use the existing town economy. Offer a gold alternative when armory supplies are scarce.

## First playable milestone

Recruit a few soldiers, fight a bandit party, recover the wounded, and promote survivors into two different roles. Take the resulting company into a second battle and check that the roles and formations behave as shown.
