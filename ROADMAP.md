# Untitled Armies roadmap

The immediate priority is the campaign loop: recruit, fight, recover, and improve the company. Side quests follow after that loop is tested in landscape on a phone.

## 1. Party and promotions

- Hire and promote selected quantities. Show available recruits, healthy troops, wounded troops, training progress, price, and wages.
- Award troop training from deployed survivors. Resolve casualties and training together once per battle.
- Give inns one treatment budget for the whole company. Show the recovery preview before resting.
- Verify that older saves retain their troop types, party size, wounds, equipment, and campaign progress.

## 2. Branching troop trees

- Pilot Valdoria first. Valdorian recruits can choose a shield, spear, or bow path. House retainers have a mounted path.
- Make each path change what troops do in battle. Verify that existing faction and tier combinations still work.
- Extend the same model to the other realms after the pilot has been played and balanced.

## 3. Deployment and reserves

- Offer an optional deployment phase and keep Quick Start.
- Choose which ready troops deploy, which wait in reserve, and where formations start on the player's side.
- Test the same setup and commands in Classic practice and campaign. Keep practice isolated from campaign saves.
- Reserves remain available for future encounters. Mid-battle reinforcements are a separate mechanic.

## 4. Weapons, armor, and loot

- Start with modular swords and spears. Weapon parts shape reach, damage, handling, and weight. Quick, Balanced, and Heavy adjust the finished weapon.
- Forge weapons in the smithy and reuse compatible recipes as troop patterns. Pay with materials and gold, or gold alone.
- Recover gear from defeated enemies, then equip, sell, or salvage it. Feed recovered materials into the existing town economy.
- Modular axes and bows extend the same crafting and loot loop. Test bow and axe presets in Classic practice.
- Tune armor tradeoffs and add more parts after playtesting. Individual weapon upgrades follow once the core crafting choices are balanced.

## Overworld releases

1. **Road reliability.** Close stale bounties, give all moving parties a fair turn at route planning, and retest town entry and pursuit in landscape. Verify player bounty payouts and existing saves.
2. **Valdorian nobles.** Add King Edric and Lady Isolde to the town menus and the roads. Give each a home, a reason to travel, and a location that persists through saves. Test meeting the same person at their seat and on the road.
3. **Bandit territories.** Pilot road robbers, forest raiders, and highland deserters. Give each a distinct roster and region. Test that patrols respond to threats and that clearing a road creates a period of safety.
4. **Trade and town news.** Follow one iron delivery from a village to a smithy. Make the destination market and local news reflect a delivery or a raid.
5. **Rollout and map readability.** Extend the tested behaviors across the realms. Check phone controls, party markers, visibility, and older saves across several campaign weeks.

## First playable milestone

Recruit a few soldiers, fight a bandit party, recover the wounded, and promote survivors into two different roles. Take the resulting company into a second battle and check that the roles and formations behave as shown.
