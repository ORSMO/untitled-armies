WEAPON MASTERY: each fighter remembers separate familiarity with shortswords, arming swords, greatswords and axes (18–97). A skilled swordsman can still be new to axes. UNIT ROLES: Infantry always prioritize melee weapons and never shoot. Archers keep their ranged weapon in hand and fire from a distance; they draw a sword only against a nearby enemy. Without ammunition, they avoid charging distant enemies. Mixed armies alternate infantry and archers. Cavalry is reserved for later.

RANGED COMBAT: V2.4 triples maximum and ideal shooting distances. Better experience and ranged familiarity produce tighter shot groups, and experienced archers lead moving targets. Archers can switch to a new enemy during an existing draw rather than repeatedly cancelling shots when their target disappears. Projectiles still travel physically and can miss. War bows carry 18 arrows (fast), crossbows carry 12 bolts (slow reload, strong piercing), and javelins carry 6 throws (short range, powerful impact). Shots follow lofted trajectories over friendly ranks and stop when they reach the ground instead of sliding along it. Low shots near the shooter or impact point can still strike soldiers. Projectiles have travel time, can miss, and interact with directional shields and armor. Experience and independent ranged-weapon familiarity improve accuracy and timing; set Ranged skill separately from melee Mastery in Test gear. Soldiers automatically switch to melee when closely threatened or out of ammunition. A shield is lowered when aiming a two-handed bow or crossbow.

IMPACT REACTIONS: Heavy hits cause knockback and stagger. A successful active block can create a counterattack opening; a weak shield can suffer a GUARD BREAK. Plate and solid shields can stop a swing and recoil the attacker.

Green and red soldiers wear mixed armor and carry different weapons. One-handed swords can use shields; greatswords and axes occupy both hands. Swing stages: wind-up → active hitbox → recovery. Your troops are still invulnerable.
